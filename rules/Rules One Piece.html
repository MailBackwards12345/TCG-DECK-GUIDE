One Piece TCG - Beginner Rules

One Piece is a trading card game where players use their Leaders, Characters and DON!! cards to battle their opponent. Your goal is to defeat your opponent's Leader.

1. Your Objective

You win by meeting either of these conditions:

  • Your opponent has no Life cards remaining and their Leader takes damage.
  • Your opponent runs out of cards in their deck.

2. Your Deck

A standard One Piece deck contains:

  • 1 Leader card: Your main card. It determines your deck's colours and starting Life.
  • 50-card deck: Contains Character, Event and Stage cards.
  • 10 DON!! cards: A separate deck used to pay costs and increase power.

You can normally include up to 4 copies of a card with the same card number. Your deck can only contain colours that match your Leader.

Card Types

  • Leader: Your main character. It can attack and use effects.
  • Character: Cards played onto your field to attack and use effects.
  • Event: Cards that provide effects when activated.
  • Stage: Cards that remain on your field and provide ongoing effects.
  • DON!!: Resources used to pay costs and increase power.

3. Setting Up the Game

  1. Place your Leader face-up in the Leader area.
  2. Shuffle your 50-card deck and place it in your deck area.
  3. Place your 10 DON!! cards face-down in the DON!! deck area.
  4. Decide who goes first using rock-paper-scissors or another agreed method.
  5. Both players draw 5 cards.
  6. Each player may mulligan once by returning their entire hand, shuffling and drawing 5 new cards.
  7. Place Life cards face-down from the top of your deck equal to your Leader's Life value.

4. Your Turn

Every turn follows five phases in this order.

1. Refresh Phase

  • Return DON!! attached to your Leader and Characters to your cost area in the rested position.
  • Set your rested cards active again.

2. Draw Phase

  • Draw 1 card from your deck.
  • The player going first does not draw on their first turn.

3. DON!! Phase

  • Take 2 DON!! cards from your DON!! deck and place them in your cost area.
  • The first player only takes 1 DON!! on their first turn.

4. Main Phase

You can perform these actions in any order and as many times as you can afford:

  • Play Character cards by paying their DON!! cost.
  • Play Stage cards by paying their DON!! cost.
  • Activate card effects.
  • Give DON!! to your Leader or Characters.
  • Attack with your Leader or Characters.
  • Activate Event cards when their timing allows.

5. End Phase

Resolve any effects that happen at the end of your turn. Then your opponent begins their turn.

5. Understanding DON!!

DON!! cards are your main resource. They have two important uses.

Paying Costs

To play a card, rest the required number of active DON!! cards in your cost area.

For example, to play a Character with a cost of 3, you must rest 3 active DON!! cards.

Increasing Power

You can give an active DON!! card to your Leader or one of your Characters. Each DON!! given provides that card with +1000 power during your turn.

Given DON!! returns to your cost area rested during your next Refresh Phase.

6. Attacking and Battles

You can attack with your Leader and any Characters that are active and allowed to attack.

Declaring an Attack

  1. Rest the card you are attacking with.
  2. Choose your opponent's Leader or one of their rested Characters as the target.
  3. Your opponent has the opportunity to use a Blocker or Counter.
  4. Compare the attacking card's power with the target's power.

If the attacking card's power is equal to or higher than the target's power, the attack succeeds.

If the attack targets a Leader, a successful attack makes your opponent take 1 Life card.

If the attack targets a Character, that Character is K.O.'d if the attack succeeds, unless an effect prevents it.

7. Counter Cards

Counter cards help you defend against attacks. You can use Counter cards from your hand during the Counter Step if they have a Counter value or an appropriate Counter effect.

Counter Example

Your opponent attacks your Leader with 6000 power. Your Leader has 5000 power.

You use a +2000 Counter card, increasing your Leader's power to 7000 for that battle.

Because your Leader now has higher power than the attack, the attack is stopped.

8. Blockers

A Blocker is a Character with an effect that allows it to redirect an attack to itself.

  • Rest the Blocker to activate its effect.
  • The Blocker becomes the new target of the attack.
  • The battle continues against the Blocker's power.

9. Life Cards and Triggers

When your Leader is hit by a successful attack, you take the top card of your Life area into your hand.

Some Life cards have a Trigger effect. When you take that card from your Life area, you may activate its Trigger instead of adding it to your hand.

Follow the instructions written on the Trigger. Some Triggers allow you to play a card or activate an effect.

10. Important Keywords

Rush

A Character with Rush can attack on the same turn it is played. Characters without Rush normally cannot attack on the turn they enter the field.

Double Attack

When a successful attack with Double Attack hits a Leader, it deals 2 Life damage instead of 1.

Banish

When a successful attack with Banish hits a Leader, the Life card is trashed instead of being added to the opponent's hand.

Blocker

Allows a Character to redirect an attack to itself by resting.

11. Field Limits

  • You can have up to 5 Characters on your field.
  • You can have 1 Stage on your field.
  • Your Leader stays in the Leader area.
  • DON!! cards stay in the cost area unless they are given to a card.

12. Helpful Tips for Beginners

  • Do not spend all your DON!! immediately. Keeping some available can give you more options.
  • Think about whether you should attack your opponent's Leader or their Characters.
  • Pay attention to your opponent's available Counter cards.
  • Read card effects carefully. They can change the normal rules.
  • Remember that both players cannot attack during their first turn.

Remember: Card effects can override the normal rules. If a card tells you to do something different, follow the card's instructions.
