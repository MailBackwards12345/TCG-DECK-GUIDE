King Ghidorah – Emperor of the Cosmos

Character Information

Hand Size: 5

Health: 33

Special: Starts the game with 3 King Ghidorah counters.

Deck Summary

King Ghidorah is built around surviving damage and making use of cards in your discard pile. His three counters give him extra chances to stay in the game, making him different from characters with a normal Health total.

His character ability lets you remove a card from your hand to remove an Attack or Foundation from your discard pile and attempt to play it that turn. This gives you another way to reuse important cards.

Recommended Momentum

0–1 Momentum

Ghidorah's main character ability does not require Momentum. You can focus more on building your Stage and keeping useful cards available in your hand and discard pile.

Stage

Recommended Stage: 8–10

A Stage of this size gives you resources to play your cards and support your attacks. You also want to keep enough cards in your hand to make use of Ghidorah's character ability.

King Ghidorah's Ability

His response allows him to change the damage of an attack before the damage step of an enemies attack, reduce it's damage to 3

His Enhance ability allows you to commit him and remove a card from your hand to remove an Attack or Foundation from your discard pile. You can then try to play that card during the same turn.

Tricky Stuff This Deck Can Do

  • Use your three counters to survive multiple times throughout the game.
  • Choose carefully which card to remove from your hand, since you need to pay that cost to use the ability.
  • Keep useful cards in your discard pile so you have options when you activate Ghidorah's ability.
  • Make your opponent work through your extra survivability before they can win.
