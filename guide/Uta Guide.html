Deck Summary

Green Uta is a Film-focused deck that aims to build a strong board using characters from the FILM category. The deck uses Uta's Leader ability to search for more FILM cards, helping you maintain resources and consistently develop your board.

The deck combines character pressure, defensive options and powerful events to control the board while steadily putting pressure on your opponent.

Tricky Stuff This Deck Can Do

Tot Musica

Tot Musica can rest an opponent's character and potentially KO a rested character. This makes it useful for dealing with troublesome characters on your opponent's board.

Bartolomeo

Bartolomeo provides a defensive option with Blocker, allowing you to protect your Leader or other important characters.

Building Your Board

Use Uta's Leader ability to find FILM cards and keep developing your board. Try to establish characters before committing to larger attacks.
