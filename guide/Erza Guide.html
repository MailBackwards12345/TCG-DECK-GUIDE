Erza Scarlet

Character Information

Hand Size: 6

Health: 31

Deck Summary

This Erza deck focuses on Spell attacks and building a strong Foundation base. Fairy Glitter and Wheel of Heaven: Scattered Petals are important attacks in the list, giving you ways to put pressure on your opponent.

The deck has a large number of Foundations, so take advantage of your resources while choosing the right time to commit them for abilities.

Recommended Momentum

2 Momentum

Keeping around 2 Momentum gives you some flexibility for effects that require it. You should still prioritise building your Stage and setting up your attacks.

Stage

Recommended Stage: 9–11

A larger Stage gives you more resources to support your attacks and defensive options. With so many Foundations in the deck, you can build up a substantial Stage over the course of the game.

Tricky Stuff This Deck Can Do

  • Use your Spell attacks to put pressure on your opponent.
  • Fairy Glitter is one of your key attacks, so make sure you use it at the right time.
  • Use your Foundations to support your attack strings and improve your options during combat.
  • Keep enough resources available to defend yourself on your opponent's turn.
