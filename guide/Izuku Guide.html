Character Information

Hand Size: 6

Deck Summary

Izuku Midoriya is a high-damage attack deck that focuses on building a large Stage and creating long strings of attacks. The deck can deal significant damage while maintaining defensive options.

Deku's character ability helps cycle through your hand by discarding and drawing cards. This allows you to find the cards you need while keeping enough defensive options available.

The deck is primarily built around the Life symbol, with a potential Good symbol option.

Recommended Momentum

3 Momentum

Why?

Three Momentum gives you access to Powerful and EX costs, while also supporting Momentum-based abilities from cards such as Gift From Mom and Without Limits.

Stage

10+ Stage

Deku benefits from having a large Stage. This helps you consistently pass checks, play multiple cards and maintain longer attack strings.

Tricky BS This Deck Can Do

Hand Cycling

Deku can use his character ability to discard and redraw cards. This helps you replace cards you don't need while searching for attacks, foundations or defensive options.

Staying Defensive

Even while cycling through your hand, you can retain defensive options. This makes it harder for your opponent to punish you for committing to an aggressive turn.
