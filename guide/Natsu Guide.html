Natsu Dragneel

Character Information

Hand Size: 6

Health: 32

Deck Summary

This is an aggressive Fairy Tail deck built around Natsu's Dragon Slayer attacks. The aim is to keep attacking, build pressure and use your attacks and foundations to support longer attack strings.

Try to keep your turns moving rather than spending too much time building your Stage. Your attacks are the main way you put pressure on your opponent.

Recommended Momentum

2 Momentum

Keeping around 2 Momentum gives you some flexibility for cards and abilities that require it. You can build Momentum by playing attacks during your turn.

Stage

Recommended Stage: 8–10

This gives you a reasonable amount of resources for your attack strings while leaving room to keep playing attacks. You do not need to focus entirely on building a huge Stage.

Tricky BS This Deck Can Do

  • Use your Dragon Slayer attacks to keep pressure on your opponent.
  • Use your Foundations to support your attacks and help keep your turns going.
  • Fairy Tail Guild and Heroic Conviction give you additional options outside of your attack cards.
  • Keep track of your resources so you do not run out of options halfway through an attack string.
