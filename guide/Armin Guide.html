Deck Summary

Armin Arlert, Power of the Colossal is a mixture of defensive and offensive gameplay. As a versatile character, he's a great pick for competitive play.

The deck is built around building a large Stage before unleashing COLOSSAL damage on your opponent, while still maintaining a strong defensive presence.

Recommended Momentum: 2

Armin Arlert, Power of the Colossal only requires 2 Momentum to pay for his EX and Powerful keywords.

This is useful because he doesn't need much Momentum. When he cycles, he can have more attacks available for "round 2".

Stage: 9–11

Yes, I know it looks like the tragic event from 2001.

Armin requires a bigger Stage because this is what allows him to build up towards his damage output.

Pre-Shift Setup

In standard gameplay, Armin wants a pre-set-up Stage before shifting. Ideally, you want 2 face-down Foundations ready to pay for the Colossal Titan's abilities later.

However, if your opponent is using a Character that relies on face-down Foundations (for example, Biollante), you should avoid playing face-down Foundations until you shift using the Colossal Titan's first Enhance.

Building Towards the Big Attack

Once you've built a decent Stage and have the right cards, you can potentially end the game in one turn.

For example, killing your opponent on turn 8 after hitting them with a 58 Speed and 47 Damage attack.

This can involve a string of 3, Go for the Win (Action), Colossal Confrontation (returned to your hand by Command of the Founder), and then Weapons Within.

The Colossal Titan – Finale

When Armin shifts, preferably after building his Stage for a massive attack string, he can still generate face-down Foundations.

However, he now generates them through an Enhance rather than a static ability.

This Form allows him to reach much higher damage outputs. Ideally, Armin should shift before beginning his big string of attacks.

Tricky Stuff Armin Can Do

Armin can use his larger Stage to pass higher checks. This can help him successfully play attacks or block incoming attacks.

Basically, his Stage makes him annoying to deal with on both offence and defence.
