Raphael – TMNT

Character Information

Hand Size

6 (Standard)

Health

32 (Standard)

Deck Summary

Raphael is an aggressive offensive deck, focused on building large attack strings and using his Fury attacks to deal huge amounts of damage.

His gameplay revolves around filling his card pool with attacks, manipulating attack speed and using his Fury attacks to finish off opponents.

Recommended Momentum: 1–2

Raphael doesn't have a built-in Momentum requirement. His main resource is the number of attack cards in his card pool, which he uses to increase or decrease his attack's speed.

Keeping 1–2 Momentum available is useful for EX and Powerful.

Stage: 6-14

Raphael wants a medium-to-large Stage, allowing him to build enough Foundations to support his attack strings while still having resources available to defend.

Tricky Stuff Raphael Can Do

Raphael can use the attacks already in his card pool to manipulate his current attack's speed, gaining +1 or -1 speed for each attack card there.

This means the longer his attack string gets, the more he can manipulate his attacks.

He can also discard his entire hand to give a Fury attack +4 damage, turning an already strong attack into a massive finisher.
