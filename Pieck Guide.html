Pieck Finger – Panzer Unit Cartman

Character Information

Hand Size: 6

Health: 29

Deck Summary

Pieck is a control-focused deck that aims to build a large Stage while managing the opponent's attacks. It can defend effectively before using its attacks to deal damage in bursts.

Her ability helps build Foundations during combat, giving you more resources to work with as the game progresses.

Recommended Momentum

1–2 Momentum

Pieck does not need a large amount of Momentum to use her main character abilities. Keeping a small amount available gives you flexibility without needing to sacrifice your entire turn to build it.

Stage

Recommended Stage: 10–12

A larger Stage gives you more options for blocking, passing checks and building your resources. This deck benefits from having enough Foundations available to support its control and attack abilities.

Cart Titan – Finale

Once you shift into Cart Titan, you gain access to its Finale ability. This helps you find Anti-Titan Artillery and build it into your Stage.

Cart Titan can also sacrifice a Foundation to adjust an attack's speed. Make sure you consider when to shift so you can get the most value from your setup.

Tricky BS This Deck Can Do

  • Build Foundations during combat using Pieck's ability.
  • Adjust an attack's damage and speed to make it harder for your opponent to defend.
  • Use Cart Titan's Finale to find Anti-Titan Artillery.
  • Manage your Foundations carefully so you can keep using your abilities.
