Sasha Blouse / Connie Springer

Character Information

Sasha Blouse: Hand Size: 6 | Health: 30

Connie Springer: Hand Size: 6 | Health: 30

Deck Summary

These two decks share a large portion of their cards, but each character has a different set of cards to support their own strategy.

Both lists use a mixture of attacks and Foundations to build resources, defend against attacks and create opportunities to deal damage.

Recommended Momentum

2 Momentum

Keeping around 2 Momentum gives you some flexibility for card effects that need it. Build Momentum through your attacks and avoid spending it without a reason.

Stage

Recommended Stage: 8–10

A Stage of this size gives you a reasonable balance between building resources and keeping cards available to play. Adjust how much you build depending on whether you are playing Sasha or Connie.

Tricky Stuff This Deck Can Do

  • Use the shared cards to build a consistent Foundation base.
  • Use your character-specific cards to support the strategy of the character you selected.
  • Keep your defensive options available instead of committing every Foundation during your own turn.
  • Choose your attack order carefully so you get the most value from your effects.
