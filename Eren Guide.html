Eren Yeager – Usurper

Character Information

Hand Size

5 (Shorter, but still viable)

Health

36 (He can take a bit of damage)

Deck Summary

Eren is a high-damage, aggressive character. The deck focuses on creating long strings of attacks, building Momentum and overwhelming your opponent with damage.

Build Momentum early, cycle through your cards and keep attacking until your opponent struggles to defend.

Recommended Momentum: 3

Three Momentum is recommended because Ymir Fritz // The Founding Titans' Power requires 3 Momentum to use its First Form and take an additional turn.

Building Momentum

Play around 2–3 attacks, then use Eren Yeager, Usurper's Enhance to mill 3 cards and add a Titan or Fury card to your Momentum.

Remember that this ability can only be used during your attack.

Stage: 8–10

Eren can operate with a decent amount of Stage. His deck mainly wants to build long strings of attacks, allowing him to deal significant damage when the cards line up.

Attack Titan – Finale

After cycling through your deck and taking some damage, shift into Attack Titan.

Think of this like changing into a boxer. Try to get 1–2 attacks in before switching to a more defensive approach.

Attack Titan gives your Titan attacks +3 damage and Stun: 1, making your attacks considerably more threatening.

Tricky BS Eren Can Do

Every time Eren takes damage, he can commit one of your opponent's Foundations.

This means that even if you half-block or take the full damage from an attack, you can potentially disrupt your opponent's Stage.
