Reiner Braun – Marley’s Shield

Character Information

Hand Size: 6

Health: 30

Deck Summary

Reiner is a defensive and aggressive deck that focuses on surviving the opponent's attacks before responding with powerful attacks of your own.

Your Foundations provide defensive options and help you maintain resources. Cards such as Scream, High Guard and Low Guard support your ability to defend, while your attack package gives you opportunities to push damage back onto your opponent.

The aim is to establish a strong board, protect your Health and choose the right moments to launch your attacks.

Recommended Momentum

2–3 Momentum

Why?

Keeping a small Momentum reserve gives you flexibility when using cards with additional costs. You should prioritise building your Stage and maintaining enough resources to defend and continue your attack strings.

Stage

9–11 Foundations

A larger Stage gives you more resources to work with. This is particularly useful for a deck that wants to balance defensive plays with opportunities to attack.

Playing Reiner

Defensive Setup

Use your defensive Foundations to help manage incoming attacks. Scream, High Guard, Low Guard and Bar the Door are important cards to keep in mind when defending.

Try not to spend every resource immediately. Keeping enough Foundations available can help you respond to your opponent's next move.

Building Pressure

Once you have established your board, start using your attacks to pressure your opponent. Your attack package contains several different options, so consider the situation before committing to a long attack string.

Using Your Resources

Cards such as Finally Free, Finding Strength and Untied Front help form part of your Foundation package. Look for opportunities to develop your Stage while keeping enough resources available for your next turn.

Tricky BS This Deck Can Do

  • Defensive flexibility: High Guard, Low Guard and Bar the Door give you defensive options when dealing with incoming attacks.
  • Building your board: Your large Foundation package gives you plenty of opportunities to establish resources.
  • Changing the pace: You can focus on defending early before switching to a more aggressive approach.
  • Attack variety: Your attacks provide several ways to pressure your opponent rather than relying on one particular attack.
