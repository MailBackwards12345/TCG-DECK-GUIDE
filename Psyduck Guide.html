Psyduck – Water / Lightning Deck

Deck Summary

A Water and Lightning deck that uses a mixture of Pokémon to attack from different angles. It has a large Energy supply, with 13 Water Energy and 12 Lightning Energy, allowing it to support both types.

The deck includes several different Pokémon rather than focusing on one evolution line. It also has Trainer cards to help with searching, drawing and keeping your Pokémon healthy.

Tricky Stuff This Deck Can Do

Fresh Water Set

Heal damage from your Pokémon to help keep them in the game.

Water and Lightning Coverage

Use two different Energy types to give yourself more attacking options.

Multiple Pokémon Options

Switch between different attackers depending on the situation.

Trainer Support

Use your Trainer cards to help find cards, improve consistency and support your Pokémon.
